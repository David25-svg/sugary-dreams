Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Sugary Dreams respects the privacy of customers and website visitors.

Information We May Collect

When you contact Sugary Dreams or place an order, you may provide information such as your name, contact information, event date, order details, delivery or pickup information and design references.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to communicate with you, prepare quotes, create and fulfill orders, coordinate pickup or delivery, provide customer service and maintain necessary business records.

Sharing

Sugary Dreams does not sell customer personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate the business or fulfill an order, or when required by law.

Photos

Sugary Dreams may photograph completed products for its portfolio, website or social media. Customers should contact Sugary Dreams if a submitted reference or finished product contains personal information they do not want displayed publicly.

Third-Party Services

If the website links to Instagram, payment providers, forms or other third-party services, those services may have their own privacy practices and policies.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have provided to Sugary Dreams, contact the business through its published contact method.
