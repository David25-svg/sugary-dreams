Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Because Sugary Dreams products are made specifically for each customer, custom orders involve advance planning, ingredients and production time.

Deposits & Payments

Any deposit or payment terms will be communicated when your order is quoted. An order is not considered confirmed until the required payment has been received.

Cancellations

Contact Sugary Dreams as soon as possible if you need to cancel. Refund eligibility depends on how much work has already been completed and whether ingredients, decorations or other custom materials have already been purchased or prepared.

Custom & Completed Orders

Completed custom products generally cannot be returned or resold. Refunds are therefore not guaranteed simply because a customer changes their mind, no longer needs the order or dislikes an approved design choice.

Problems With an Order

If there is a problem caused by Sugary Dreams, contact us as soon as possible and provide photos when appropriate. We will review the situation and determine an appropriate resolution, which may include a partial refund, replacement or other remedy depending on the circumstances.

After Pickup

Sugary Dreams is not responsible for damage caused after pickup by improper handling, transportation, storage, heat, accidents or other conditions outside our control.

Missed Pickup

Failure to collect an order at the agreed time does not automatically qualify the order for a refund.
